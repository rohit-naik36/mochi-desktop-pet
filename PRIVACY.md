# Mochi privacy policy

_Last updated: 23 October 2026_

Mochi is a desktop pet that keeps you company while you work. This policy explains what it stores and what it doesn't.

## The short version

Mochi does not collect, send or sell any of your data. It works fully offline. Nothing you do in Mochi leaves your computer.

## What Mochi stores on your computer

To remember things between sessions, Mochi keeps one file on your own PC (in your Windows user profile, inside Mochi's app data folder). It contains:

- the name you asked Mochi to call you, your buddy's look, name and gender, and your language
- your settings (reminders, wandering, where the buddy sits on screen)
- your buddy's happiness, hunger and energy
- points, badges, streak days and activity counts
- your daily mood check-ins (a number from 1 to 5 per day)
- the "good things" you put in the gratitude jar

Worries you type into **Feed me a worry** are never saved. Mochi reads them on your computer to write a reply, then forgets them.

## What Mochi does not do

- No internet connection: no accounts, servers, analytics, tracking, advertising or cloud sync.
- No access to your files, camera, microphone, location, contacts or other apps.
- No data shared with anyone, including the developer.

## Your control

Uninstalling Mochi removes the app. To delete your saved data too, delete Mochi's app data folder. You can also change or clear your name at any time from Mochi's right-click menu.

## Safety

Mochi is a friendly companion, not a medical or mental health service. If you are thinking about suicide or self-harm, or are in danger, please contact Tele-MANAS (14416), KIRAN (1800-599-0019), Vandrevala Foundation (+91 9999 666 555) or emergency services (112) in India.

## Children

Mochi does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
