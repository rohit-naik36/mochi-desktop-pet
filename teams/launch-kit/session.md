# Intro session (20 minutes)

For Little Smoke Studio to run with HR, live or on a video call. Record it
for people who can't join.

## Run sheet

| Time | What | Who |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Welcome, why [Company] is doing this | HR |
| 0:03 | Meet Mochi: a live tour on a shared screen | Little Smoke Studio |
| 0:10 | Privacy: what HR can and can't see | Little Smoke Studio |
| 0:14 | Where to get help, any time | HR |
| 0:16 | Questions | Both |

## What to say

**Why (HR, 2 minutes).** Long days, back-to-back calls, deadlines. The small habits go first: water, breaks, sleep. Mochi is a gentle nudge for those, and a way to make our support easier to find.

**The tour (7 minutes).** Show, don't list:

1. A water reminder pops up. Click Done, then stay at the keyboard: Mochi notices it wasn't a real break.
2. Right-click: **How I feel today**, the **Gratitude jar**, **Focus with me**.
3. **Big call coming up**: the pep talk, and "how did it go?" afterwards.
4. **Feed me a worry**: type "deadline tomorrow and I'm behind". Show the reply and the next small step. Then type something heavy and show our support contacts appearing first.
5. Open **Mochi for Teams**: support contacts, "your week", this week's campaign and question.

**Privacy (4 minutes).** Say it plainly, then show it:

- What you type to Mochi never leaves your laptop.
- If you choose, Mochi adds counts (not words) to your team's totals once a week, with no name or device ID.
- HR sees totals only, for groups of 8 or more, rounded to 5. Show the Pulse privacy panel.
- Open **Privacy > See exactly what's added this week** in Mochi for Teams.
- It's your choice, and you can change it any time.

**Help (HR, 2 minutes).** EAP [name and number], HR partner [name], POSH Internal Committee [contact], and Tele-MANAS 14416 (free, 24/7).

## Questions people ask

**Can my manager see what I type?** No. Nothing you type leaves your laptop, for anyone.

**Does it track my screen, keystrokes or apps?** No. It never reads your screen, what you type in other apps, window titles or websites. It only knows whether the keyboard or mouse was used in the last few seconds (to tell a real break), and whether the mic or camera is on (to stay quiet on calls). There's an optional setting, off unless you turn it on, that lets Mochi see the name of the app in front (like "Excel") for a playful line; that never leaves your laptop either.

**Can I turn it off?** You can mute it for 30 minutes, an hour or until you turn it back on, change how chatty it is, or quit it from the tray menu.

**Is the AI sending my words to the cloud?** No. Smarter replies run on your laptop itself; nothing is sent anywhere.

**What if I'm going through something serious?** Mochi will show you our EAP and national helplines straight away. Please reach out to them; they are there for exactly this. In an emergency, call 112.

**I work nights.** Right-click Mochi and choose **Sleep schedule > I work night shifts**, and it will follow your hours.
