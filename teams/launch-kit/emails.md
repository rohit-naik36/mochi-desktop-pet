## 1. Announcement (one week before)

**Subject:** Meet Mochi, a small desk buddy for long work days

Hi everyone,

From [launch date], [Company] is giving everyone in [teams] a little desk buddy called Mochi.

Mochi sits in the corner of your screen and helps with the small things that slip on busy days: a sip of water, a stretch, a break for your eyes, a moment to breathe before a big call. It goes quiet by itself when you're on a call. If a day gets heavy, you can tell Mochi what's on your mind, and it will show you our support contacts, including [EAP name] on [EAP number].

**Your privacy.** What you type to Mochi never leaves your laptop. Not to us, not to anyone. If you choose to, Mochi can add your weekly counts (for example, how many breaks were taken) to your team's totals. HR sees totals only, for groups of 8 or more people, never names, never words, never one person's numbers. You can see exactly what would be shared, and turn it off any time.

Mochi will appear on your laptop on [launch date]. Nothing to install.

[Name]
[Role]

## 2. Launch day

**Subject:** Mochi is here 👋

Hi everyone,

Mochi is now on your laptop. Look for the little buddy near the bottom of your screen (or the Mochi icon in the system tray).

A few things to try today:

- Right-click Mochi and choose **How I feel today**.
- When a water reminder pops up, actually stand up. Mochi notices 😄
- Before your next big meeting, choose **Big call coming up**.
- Open **Mochi for Teams** from the tray menu to see our support contacts and your own week with Mochi.

The first time Mochi starts, it asks whether you'd like to add your counts to your team's totals. It's completely up to you, and you can change your mind in Privacy.

Join our 20-minute intro session on [date and time]: [link].

[Name]

## 4. Note for managers

**Subject:** Mochi: three ways you can help your team

Hi [manager],

Mochi works best when people see it's okay to use it. Three small things make a big difference:

1. **Take your breaks where people can see.** "Back in five, Mochi says stretch" does more than any poster.
2. **Leave a gap between meetings.** Mochi suggests a stretch after long calls; it helps if the next one doesn't start straight away.
3. **Don't ask who opted in.** Team totals are anonymous on purpose. Please never ask anyone whether they share their counts, or what they told Mochi.

You'll see your team's trends in our monthly people update, as totals only.

Thanks,
[Name]
