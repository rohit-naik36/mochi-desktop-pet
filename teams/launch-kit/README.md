# Mochi for Teams: launch kit

Everything a company needs to introduce Mochi to its people. Little Smoke
Studio fills in the brackets with the company and runs the intro session
with HR.

| File | What it is | Who uses it |
|---|---|---|
| `emails.md` | Four ready-to-send messages: the announcement, launch day, a two-week nudge, and a note for managers | HR or internal comms |
| `session.md` | A 20-minute intro session: run sheet, what to say, and answers to the questions people ask | Little Smoke Studio with HR |
| `poster.html` | An A4 poster for pantries and lifts. Open it in a browser and print, or save as PDF | Admin or facilities |

## Launch timeline

| When | What |
|---|---|
| 2 weeks before | We set up the company (contacts, teams, welcome note) and send IT the rollout guide |
| 1 week before | IT pilots on 5 to 10 laptops. HR sends the **announcement** email. Posters go up |
| Launch day | IT rolls out to the group. HR sends the **launch day** email. Intro session (live or recorded) |
| Week 1 | First wellness campaign, often **Calm week** or **Hydration week** |
| Week 2 | HR sends the **two-week nudge**. Managers get their note |
| Week 3 | First weekly question, for example "How manageable was your workload this week?" |
| End of month 1 | First look at Mochi Pulse together on a call |
| End of the pilot | Pilot report from Pulse, and a decision |

## Three things to get right

1. **Say plainly what HR can and can't see.** Trust is the whole product. Use the exact privacy wording in these templates.
2. **Managers go first.** People take breaks when they see their manager take one.
3. **No pressure to opt in.** Adding counts to team totals is a choice. Never chase people about it, and never ask who did.
