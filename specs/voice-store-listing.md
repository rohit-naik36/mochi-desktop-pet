# Voice input: Store listing and certification notes (paste into Partner Center at release)

**What's new (0.13.0), short:** You can now talk to Mochi. Turn on Voice input in Settings, then hold a button to speak a worry instead of typing it. English only. Off by default. It all happens on your PC, and Mochi does not speak back.

**Privacy declarations to update:**
- Microphone capability is declared in the package (`microphone`). Reason shown to reviewers: push-to-talk for the Feed me a worry window; used only while the user holds the button; audio is processed on the device and never stored or transmitted.
- Network: one optional download of the speech pack (about 295 MB) from GitHub releases after the user consents.
- Privacy policy URL is unchanged (https://mochicuddle.com/privacy.html); the page is updated in the same release.

**Certification risks:** the microphone capability can only be confirmed by a real submission. Submit early and keep the privacy text above ready.
