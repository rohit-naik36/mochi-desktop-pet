Mochi for teams

A wellbeing buddy for every desk in your company

Long hours, deadlines and night shifts wear people down quietly. Mochi gives every employee a small, friendly reason to drink water, stretch, rest their eyes and talk about what is heavy, without anyone watching over their shoulder.

What your company gets

Everything in the free app, set up for your people

Your helplines, built in

Show your Employee Assistance Programme, HR contact and Internal Committee (POSH) details right where people need them, next to the national helplines.

Licensed for every seat

One licence key for the whole company, easy rollout to every laptop, and the reminders and schedule set to match your shifts.

Anonymous team insights

See, as team totals only, whether break habits and focus time are improving. Never anyone's worries, moods or name.

Made for shift work

Night-shift aware, in English, Hindi and Hinglish, so support, operations and BPO teams across India get reminders on their own hours.

Smarter replies, kept private

A small AI built into the company edition writes more personal replies on the laptop itself. Nothing goes to a cloud AI, so there is nothing new for IT to approve. English for now.

Your branding

A welcome message from your company and your wellbeing programme named in the app.

Privacy first

Employees trust it because it doesn't report on them

Mochi was built to keep everything personal on the employee's own laptop. That stays true for companies.

  • Worries typed into Mochi are never saved or sent anywhere
  • Moods and gratitude notes stay on the device
  • Team insights are anonymous totals, with a minimum group size
  • The built-in AI runs on the laptop, never in the cloud
  • No screen monitoring, no keystroke logging, no tracking of what people work on
