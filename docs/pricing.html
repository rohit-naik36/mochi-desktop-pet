Pricing questions

Will the free app always be free?

Yes. The personal app on the Microsoft Store stays free, with no ads and no paid upgrades inside it.

How is Mochi for teams priced?

Per employee per year, based on team size. Tell us about your company and we will send a quote.

Can we try it first?

Yes. We start with a pilot for one team so you can see how people use it before rolling it out.
