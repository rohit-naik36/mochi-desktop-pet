Meet the buddies

Pick the buddy that feels like you

Every buddy cares about the same things: your water, your back, your eyes and how you are really doing. They just show it differently.

Um... would you drink some water? For me? 💧

Shy and sweet

Mochi

The original black mochi with glowing white eyes. Speaks softly, blushes when you pet it, and when you skip a reminder it quietly turns away and pouts instead of making a scene.

WATER BREAK! Champions hydrate! 💧🔥

Fiery hype coach

Cinder

A toasty charcoal mochi with ember eyes and drifting sparks. Bouncy, loud and all heart. Spins when you win and flares up red if you ignore a water break.

Rivers need rain. So do you 💧

Calm and wise

Mist

Silver fog with dark eyes, the easiest to see on dark wallpapers. Drifts slowly, glows when it is happy, and sighs and fades instead of fuming.

Water break! Earth is 71% water. You should be at least a bit 💧

Dreamy night owl

Dusk

An indigo mochi full of tiny twinkling stars and space facts. Groggy in the morning, lively at night, twirls when happy and dims its stars when it sulks.

You've got this 💗 One sip of water first?

Warm and hopeful

Hope Coming soon

A pink mochi with warm rosy eyes. Always finds the bright side, floats little hearts when you take a break, and when you skip one it just looks at you, hopefully, until you do.

The serious things stay the same

Every buddy has its own voice for reminders, greetings and jokes. When someone shares something heavy, like sadness, harm or a crisis, every buddy answers with the same careful words and the same helplines. Personality never gets in the way of safety.
