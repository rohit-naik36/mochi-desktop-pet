Features

Everything your buddy can do

Mochi is small, but it does a lot. Here is the full tour.

Get it free on Microsoft Store

Healthy reminders

Water every 45 minutes, a stretch every hour and an eye rest every 20 minutes. Click Done and your buddy cheers. Ignore a reminder for 3 minutes and it floats to the middle of your screen and sulks until you take the break, or snooze it.

  • Turn each reminder on or off from the menu
  • Reminders wait while you focus, sleep or step away
  • Your buddy gets hungry too, so feed it a snack now and then

Feed me a worry

Type what is on your mind. Your buddy eats it letter by letter, reflects back what you said, gives comfort that fits the topic and suggests one small next step. It runs fully offline and nothing is saved.

  • Understands deadlines, work, study, money, people, health and self-doubt
  • Sad feelings get a soft, quiet reply with no jokes
  • Harassment worries point to 112, the Women Helpline 181 and your POSH committee

Celebrates your good news

Not everything is a worry. Type "I got promoted!" or "promotion mil gaya!" and your buddy throws a confetti party, gives you bonus points and offers to keep it in your gratitude jar.

  • Wins earn their own badges
  • Good news goes in the jar so you can find it on a rough day

Focus with me

Pick 25 or 50 minutes. A small timer appears under your buddy and it goes quiet: no chatter, no hunger and no reminders until your break. When time is up it celebrates and offers a 5-minute break.

  • Pet it during focus and it whispers "shh"
  • Asks if you want another round after the break

Sleeps when you sleep

Your buddy goes to bed at 11 pm, or 10, 12 or 1 if you prefer. If you are still working a while later, it asks whether you work night shifts, then keeps your hours instead. It dozes off when you are away for 30 minutes, and asks how you slept when you come back.

  • Made for night shifts and late deadlines
  • Reminders never pile up while you are away

Play room

Turn your desktop into a play area for a short break. Breathe with me (4 seconds in, 4 hold, 6 out), pop soft bubbles with no timer or score, or chase a warm light around the screen.

  • Every game makes your buddy happier
  • Press Esc and you are back at work

Points, badges and the achievement board

Every healthy habit earns points, with a daily cap so it stays honest. Level up, keep your streak alive and collect 26 badges. The board shows your mood for the week and your gratitude jar. Shake it for a random good memory.

  • Daily mood check-in with five faces
  • One good thing a day in the gratitude jar

Three languages

English, Hindi and Hinglish everywhere: menus, reminders, chatter and play. In Feed me a worry, your buddy replies in the language you wrote in.

Boy, girl or non-binary

Choose who your buddy is. In Hindi and Hinglish it changes how your buddy talks about itself.

Kind, with limits

Venting with a swear word gets empathy. Being rude to your buddy gets two warnings, then it hides for five minutes. Light sweet talk gets blushes, explicit talk gets a firm no.
