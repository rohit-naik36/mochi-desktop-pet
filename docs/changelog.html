What's new

Every version of Mochi

Your buddy keeps learning new things. Store users get each update automatically.

  1. v0.10

    24 Oct 2026

    • Every buddy now has its own personality, lines and animations
    • Mochi is shy, Cinder is a hype coach, Mist is calm and Dusk is a dreamy night owl
    • Available on the Microsoft Store

  2. v0.9

    24 Oct 2026

    • Sleep schedule: your buddy goes to bed at night and asks how you slept
    • Night-shift mode keeps your hours instead
    • Good news gets a confetti party and a place in the gratitude jar
    • Gentler replies for sadness and hopeless feelings

  3. v0.8

    23 Oct 2026

    • Focus timer for 25 or 50 minutes
    • Daily mood check-in and gratitude jar
    • Points, levels, streaks, 26 badges and the achievement board
    • Security hardening and privacy policy

  4. v0.7

    23 Oct 2026

    • Feed me a worry gets its own space
    • About 950 lines, with no repeats in a row
    • Your buddy reacts to where you drop it

  5. v0.6

    23 Oct 2026

    • Choose whether your buddy is a boy, a girl or non-binary
    • Sweet replies to sweet talk, a kind no to anything explicit

  6. v0.5

    23 Oct 2026

    • Hindi and Hinglish
    • Understands venting versus rudeness

  7. v0.4

    23 Oct 2026

    • Ignored reminders make your buddy upset until you take the break
    • Your buddy learns your name
    • Indian helplines for hard moments

  8. v0.2

    23 Oct 2026

    • Play room: breathe with me, bubble pop and chase the light

  9. v0.1

    23 Oct 2026

    • Mochi is born, with water, stretch and eye reminders
