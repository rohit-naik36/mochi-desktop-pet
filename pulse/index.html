What HR can and cannot see

The same rules apply in every Mochi for Teams account.

Shared, as totals only

  • Mood check-in levels, counted per group
  • Worry chat themes, such as "deadlines", never the words
  • Reminders shown and acted on, focus sessions, feature use
  • How often Mochi showed support contacts

Never leaves the laptop

  • Anything a person types to Mochi
  • Names, emails, device IDs or any single person's mood
  • Exact times or which day one person was low

Safeguards

  • Each person opts in and can preview exactly what is sent
  • Groups under 8 people are hidden
  • Counts are rounded to the nearest 5
  • No one at the company can drill down to a person
